    THE CULTUREFORGE WORKFLOW

    Find the idea.
    Then test it.

    Every direction is composed from places Qloo actually returned — and then put on trial. The evidence can come back with a no, and the order the directions are shown in changes when it does.

    01 / FIND

    Three directions, from real places.

    Your audience resolves into cultural signals, the neighbourhood returns real venues, and each direction names the venue it was built from.

    02 / TEST

    The evidence gets a vote.

    Qloo ranks the places for this audience’s signals, and compares each one with the rest of the neighbourhood. A direction can come out contested.

    03 / DECIDE

    The order changes.

    Supported first, contested last — with the reason in plain English, and the limits of the test stated on the screen.

    Live Qloo data. Every number on screen is one the API returned, and the checks say plainly what they do and do not measure.

    WHAT THE TEST ACTUALLY ASKS

    Two checks.
    Both can fail.

    A direction is a hypothesis until the local evidence has had a look at it. These are the questions, asked of the places Qloo returned.

    CHECK 01

    Does the audience pull toward this place?

    Qloo ranks the neighbourhood’s real venues for this audience’s cultural signals. A direction built on the place that ranks last is a direction the audience does not pull toward.

    One Qloo call for the whole set
    CHECK 02

    Is there room, or a fight?

    The place is compared with the rest of the neighbourhood. A place that carries only what every place here carries is the same crowd in a different room.

    One Qloo call per direction
    THE LIMIT

    What this does not measure.

    Demand, competition, rent, footfall and willingness to pay. These checks establish local cultural fit and nothing else, and the screen says so.

    Stated on every verdict

    The verdict is supported, contested, or unproven — by the evidence, with the reason attached.
